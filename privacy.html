Privacy Policy

RoastBox by Noxhel Studios · Effective date: October 24, 2026

The short version
  • No account, email, phone number or password. You just play.
  • To score guesses and write roasts, the text you type in a round is sent over HTTPS to our server, which passes it to a third-party AI model.
  • The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first.
  • Settings and your current game stay on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

RoastBox (package io.roastbox.app, the “App”) is a pass-the-phone party game published by Noxhel Studios (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions or requests, write to noxhelstudios@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

Game content you type. This means player nicknames, questions, secret “truths” and guesses. During a round it is kept on your device. When the round reaches the judging step, this text is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our proxy, which runs on Cloudflare Workers. The proxy forwards it to a third-party AI model provider (currently Google’s Gemini API) so the AI can score each guess and write a roast. We do not ask you for real names or any personal details, and you should not type sensitive information (for example health data, ID numbers, passwords, or details about other people that they would not want shared). If the AI service is unreachable, the App scores the round with an offline fallback on your device instead.

We do not create user profiles, we do not link this text to an account (there are none), and we do not use it for advertising. Our proxy does not store it in a database of ours, and request logging is turned off on our Cloudflare Worker. Like any web service, Cloudflare and the AI provider still receive technical request data (such as your IP address). We use Google’s Gemini API on its paid tier. Under Google’s terms for paid services, Google does not use this text to improve its products and processes it under its data processing terms, but it may log prompts and responses for a limited time to detect and prevent abuse. Please still do not type personal or sensitive information.

Data stored on your device. The App saves your language, sound and vibration settings, selected question packs, the time until a pack unlocked with a rewarded ad expires, whether you have seen the tutorial, and the state of the current game (players, scores, round). This stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it with Settings → Reset current game, by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Advertising data (Android). The App shows rewarded and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect against fraud in ads, Google may collect your Android advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We cap ad content at a “Teen” rating. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App shows a consent form before ads load, and you can revisit your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Google’s practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Rating prompt. If the App asks you to rate it, that uses Google Play’s in-app review dialog. Google handles your review. We do not receive anything beyond what Google Play shows developers.

Messages to us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply.

What we do not collect. We do not collect accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or precise location. The App requests only the Internet and network-state permissions, plus the advertising ID permission used by AdMob.

4. Who receives data

  • Cloudflare hosts the proxy that handles AI requests.
  • An AI model provider (currently Google Gemini) processes the game text to produce scores and roasts. The provider handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. For the paid Gemini API, those terms say Google does not use the text to improve its products.
  • Google AdMob serves ads and receives the advertising data described above.
  • Google Play distributes the App and runs the review dialog.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information. In some US states, ad-related data flows to Google may count as “sharing” for advertising; you can limit this through Settings → Ad privacy choices and Android’s ad settings.

5. International transfers

Cloudflare, Google and the AI provider operate globally, so your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, these providers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

6. How long we keep data

  • Data on your device stays until you clear it, reset the game or uninstall the App.
  • We do not keep game text in our own database, and we keep no request logs for it. Google may keep prompts and responses it receives through the paid Gemini API for a limited period to detect abuse and meet legal duties, under its own terms.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google under Google’s policies.
  • Emails to us are kept as long as needed to answer and to meet legal duties.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, withdraw consent at any time, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In Türkiye, KVKK Article 11 gives you similar rights, including asking whether your data is processed and requesting deletion, and you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK). California residents have the right to know, delete and opt out of sale or sharing, and we do not discriminate for using these rights.

Because the App has no accounts, we usually cannot tell which data belongs to you, and most of it sits on your own device, where you control it. To make a request, email noxhelstudios@gmail.com. We will answer within the time the law sets. Ad choices: Settings → Ad privacy choices in the App, and Android’s advertising ID controls.

8. Children

RoastBox is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. In some countries the minimum age is higher; if the law where you live requires a parent’s permission, please ask them first. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to noxhelstudios@gmail.com and we will act on it.

9. AI-generated content

Scores and roasts are produced by AI for entertainment only. They can be wrong, blunt, or offensive, and they are not advice or fact. Please keep the game friendly.

10. Security

We send game text over HTTPS and keep what we handle to a minimum. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and we will post the new version at this address.

2. İşlediğimiz bilgiler

Yazdığın oyun içeriği. Oyuncu takma adları, sorular, gizli “gerçekler” ve tahminler. Tur sürerken bu içerik cihazında durur. Tur puanlama aşamasına gelince bu metin, şifreli HTTPS bağlantısıyla Cloudflare Workers üzerinde çalışan aracı sunucumuza gönderilir. Aracı sunucu bunu, yapay zekânın her tahmini puanlayıp roast yazabilmesi için üçüncü taraf bir yapay zekâ model sağlayıcısına (şu anda Google Gemini API) iletir. Senden gerçek ad ya da kişisel bilgi istemiyoruz. Hassas bilgi (sağlık verisi, kimlik numarası, şifre, başkalarının paylaşılmasını istemeyeceği bilgiler gibi) yazma. Yapay zekâ servisine ulaşılamazsa Uygulama turu cihazındaki çevrimdışı yedek yöntemle puanlar.

Bu metinden kullanıcı profili oluşturmuyoruz, bir hesaba bağlamıyoruz (hesap yok) ve reklam için kullanmıyoruz. Aracı sunucumuz bunu kendi veritabanımızda saklamaz ve Cloudflare Worker’ımızda istek kaydı (log) kapalıdır. Her web hizmetinde olduğu gibi Cloudflare ve yapay zekâ sağlayıcısı yine de teknik istek verilerini (ör. IP adresi) alır. Google’ın Gemini API’sini ücretli katmanda kullanıyoruz. Google’ın ücretli hizmetler için koşullarına göre Google bu metni ürünlerini geliştirmek için kullanmaz ve veri işleme koşullarına göre işler, ancak kötüye kullanımı tespit etmek ve önlemek için istek ve yanıtları sınırlı bir süre kaydedebilir. Yine de lütfen kişisel veya hassas bilgi yazma.

Cihazında saklanan veriler. Uygulama dilini, ses ve titreşim ayarlarını, seçili soru paketlerini, ödüllü reklamla açılan bir paketin ne zaman kapanacağını, eğitimi görüp görmediğini ve mevcut oyunun durumunu (oyuncular, puanlar, tur) kaydeder. Bunlar cihazında kalır, bize gönderilmez. Ayarlar → Mevcut oyunu sıfırla ile, Android ayarlarından Uygulama verilerini temizleyerek ya da Uygulamayı kaldırarak silebilirsin.

Reklam verileri (Android). Uygulama, Google AdMob ile ödüllü ve geçiş (interstitial) reklamları gösterir. Reklamları sunmak, ölçmek ve sahtekârlığı önlemek için Google; Android reklam kimliğini, IP adresini (yaklaşık konum çıkarılabilir), cihaz ve uygulama bilgilerini ve reklamlarla etkileşimini toplayabilir. Reklam içeriğini “Teen” (genç) düzeyiyle sınırlıyoruz. Yasanın onay gerektirdiği yerlerde (örneğin AEA, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre) reklamlar yüklenmeden önce Uygulama bir onay formu gösterir ve seçimini sonradan Ayarlar → Reklam gizlilik tercihleri bölümünden değiştirebilirsin. Reklam kimliğini Android ayarlarından da sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsin (Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar). Google’ın uygulamaları için: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites ve policies.google.com/privacy.

Puanlama isteği. Uygulama senden puan istediğinde Google Play’in uygulama içi değerlendirme penceresini kullanır. Yorumunu Google yönetir. Google Play’in geliştiricilere gösterdiğinin ötesinde bir şey almayız.
